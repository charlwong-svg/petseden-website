Keeping my Rabbit Clean & Healthy

Nails & Pawpads

Rabbit nails grow surprisingly fast. Even free-roaming rabbits at home still require a nail trim and pawpad shave every month.

Shedding

Rabbits have a very thick double-coat, shedding fur every day and more in hot weather. Weekly brushing helps reduce shedding at home.

Ears

In our hot & humid climate, ears need cleaning once a month on average. Lop-eared bunnies tend to need more frequent cleaning.

Hygiene Area

Shaving the hygiene area regularly helps prevent stains around the house � applies to both short and long hair bunnies.

Wet Shower or Dry Bath?

Most rabbits are fairly clean and a dry bath usually suffices, but occasional showers help them get used to the process as they age.

Haircut

Long hair breeds require a monthly haircut to keep them looking cute & fluffy.
