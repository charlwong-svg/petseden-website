How Sponsorship Works

Sponsor a Groomer in Training

Sponsor someone through our Professional course and they work for you as a full-time groomer the day they qualify, bonded to you for two years. Sponsor a member of your own staff, or let us find candidates for you to choose from.

  1. You sign up

    Tell us whether you’re sponsoring your own staff member or would like us to find someone.

  2. We find candidates

    If you need someone, we interview interested students first and send you only the ones worth meeting. Skip this step if you already have your person.

  3. You choose

    You interview and decide. Nothing is committed until you do.

  4. They train

    Daily class with us for 3 months. You cover the course fees and provide an allowance while they train.

  5. They work for you

    They start as a full-time groomer on full salary, on a 2-year bond.

What you get

  • A groomer trained by Pets Eden, in dog, cat or rabbit grooming
  • Sponsor your own staff member, or pick from candidates we find for you
  • You interview before committing — nothing is decided for you
  • Bonded to you for 2 years once they qualify
  • Open to salons, boarding and daycare facilities, and owners still setting up

What you commit to

  • The course fees
  • Their pay while they attend class for 3 months — an allowance, or their existing wage if they already work for you
  • A full-time salary once they start grooming for you

Exact pay and bond terms are agreed between you and the person you sponsor before training begins.
