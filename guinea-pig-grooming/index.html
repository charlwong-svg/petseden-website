Keeping my Guinea Pig Clean & Healthy

Nails

Guinea pig nails grow continuously and can curl painfully if left untrimmed. A nail trim every 4-6 weeks keeps them comfortable and walking normally.

Shedding

Guinea pigs shed moderately year-round, a little more during seasonal changes. Regular brushing helps reduce loose fur at home.

Ears

Ears should be checked and gently cleaned periodically, especially in our humid climate, to avoid wax build-up or infection.

Scent Gland

Guinea pigs (especially males) have a grease/scent gland near the rump that can build up oil and debris if not cleaned regularly.

Bathing

Guinea pigs need a bath at every grooming session to properly clean their scent glands, though we keep the process quick and gentle since they can be prone to stress and chills.

Haircut

Short-haired breeds (like American or Abyssinian) rarely need trims. Long-haired breeds (like Peruvian or Silkie) need regular haircuts to stay tangle-free.
