Plaque & Tartar Removal Service

What is Plaque and Tartar?

Plaque is a sticky, colorless film of bacteria that constantly forms on our teeth and along the gum line. Plaque contains bacteria that cause cavities and gum disease. As plaque forms and is not removed by proper brushing and flossing, it can harden into tartar — sometimes called calculus — which is calcified plaque that attaches to the enamel, as well as below the gum line.

Plaque

A sticky, colorless film of bacteria that constantly forms on the teeth and along the gum line.

What it causes...
  • Canine Bad Breath
  • Stained, Discolored Teeth

Tartar

Any plaque not removed hardens into tartar — calcified plaque that attaches to the enamel and below the gum line.

What it does...
  • Teeth decay
  • Bacterial inflammation of the gums and jaws
  • Swollen jaws and gums
  • Loss of teeth
