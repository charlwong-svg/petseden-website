Keeping my Dog Clean & Healthy

Shower & Dry

Maintaining skin & coat requires regular care. After a shower, dry thoroughly with a hairdryer — dampness often leads to bacteria and yeast overgrowth.

Shedding

Most dogs are double-coated, shedding fur every day and more in hot weather. Weekly brushing helps reduce shedding at home.

Ears

In our hot & humid climate, dog ears need cleaning once a week. Irregular cleaning often leads to ear infections.

Nails & Pawpads

Dog nails grow surprisingly fast — even daily-walked dogs still need a nail trim and pawpad shave every 1–2 weeks.

Hygiene Area

Shaving the hygiene area regularly helps prevent stains around the house. Some breeds need weekly/bi-weekly shaves, others monthly.

Haircut

For some breeds, fur grows continuously. Most breeds require a haircut once a month to stay cute & fluffy.

The Importance of having a Regular Groomer

Regular grooming is essential for your dog's well-being. It allows them to become familiar with the environment and the people involved in their care, making the process more enjoyable. Groomers who know your dog can easily spot changes and alert you to potential health issues.

How Often should we send our dogs for Grooming?

If you're confident drying your dog 100% after a shower, consider our monthly grooming service. If not, our weekly or bi-weekly package is the perfect solution to keep your furry friend clean and healthy.
