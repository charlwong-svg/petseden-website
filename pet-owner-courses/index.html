For Pet Owners

Learn To Groom Your Own Pet

Short, one-to-one classes for owners who’d rather groom their own pet at home — or for the family member or helper who looks after them. No experience needed: bring your dog, cat or rabbit and learn on them, with a professional groomer beside you.

Dog Course Cat Course Rabbit Course

How It Works

  1. Pick Your Sessions

    Take a single session, or the full set at a reduced price.

  2. Bring Your Pet

    You learn on your own pet, one-to-one with a trainer. No tools to buy.

  3. Groom At Home

    Leave able to handle grooming yourself, with a Certificate of Attendance.

Good To Know

Booking & Payment

  • A 50% deposit confirms your spot; the balance is due when the course starts
  • Changing your date needs at least 3 days’ notice, or extra charges may apply
  • Prices are per participant, in Singapore Dollars
  • SkillsFuture is not applicable to these courses
  • No toolbox purchase is required, though our Basic Set is available if you’d like your own tools at home
