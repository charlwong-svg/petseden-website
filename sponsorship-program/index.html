Scholarship & Sponsorship Program

Train as a Professional Groomer — Fully Sponsored

A sponsoring salon pays your course fees and an allowance while you train, then hires you full time as a groomer. Open to students keen on our Professional Dog, Cat or Rabbit Groomer course.

I'm a Student I'm a Salon / Sponsor

How It Works

  1. Apply

    Students and sponsors each sign up using the forms below.

  2. Interview with Pets Eden

    We meet shortlisted students for a first interview.

  3. Interview with the Sponsor

    Students who pass then meet the potential employer / sponsor.

  4. 3 Months of Training

    Chosen students attend class every day for 3 months, with course fees and an allowance paid by the sponsor.

  5. Posted to the Salon

    After the course, you start work as a full-time groomer at the sponsor's salon on full salary.

For Students

  • Your course fees are paid by the sponsor
  • An allowance while you attend class
  • A full-time groomer job waiting for you, on full salary
  • Train in dog, cat or rabbit grooming — see our courses

Commitment: daily class for 3 months, then a 2-year bond with the sponsor's salon.

For Sponsors & Salons

  • You sponsor the course fees and the student's allowance during training
  • You pay full salary once they start work at your salon
  • You interview the student yourself before committing
  • A groomer trained by Pets Eden, bonded to you for 2 years

Open to: established salons, and owners who are still setting up a shop.

Exact allowance, salary and bond terms are agreed between the student and the sponsor during the process.
