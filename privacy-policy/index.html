Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Pets Eden ("we", "us", "our") runs a pet grooming salon at Blk 551 Bedok North Ave 1 #01-552, Singapore 460551. This policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it and the choices you have. It covers our website (petseden.net), our online booking page, our WhatsApp line (9227 7915) and the automated WhatsApp booking assistant that answers on it. We handle personal data in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

1. What we collect

  • Contact details: your name and handphone / WhatsApp number.
  • Pet details: your pet's name, type, breed, size, and any notes you give us for the groomer (for example health, behaviour or coat condition).
  • Booking details: the services, dates and times you book, changes and cancellations, membership and course enquiries, and our records of grooming done.
  • Pick-up address: only if you book our pet transport service.
  • Messages: the content of WhatsApp messages you send us, including messages to our booking assistant, and any photos you choose to send.
  • Applications: if you apply through our Join Us or Partner With Us forms, the details you enter (such as name, phone, age and employment history).
  • Website usage data: our website uses Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics and Google Ads conversion tracking, which use cookies and similar technology to collect information such as pages visited, device and browser type, approximate location and how you arrived at our site.

We do not collect or store your bank or card details. Payment by PayNow is handled by your bank.

2. How we use it

  • To take, confirm, change and cancel bookings, and to groom and care for your pet.
  • To arrange pet pick-up and drop-off if you book transport.
  • To answer your questions and send you booking confirmations and reminders on WhatsApp.
  • To keep records of your pet's grooming history and preferences so we can serve you better next time.
  • To process job and partner applications.
  • To understand how our website is used and to measure our advertising.
  • To meet our legal and accounting obligations.

We will only send you promotional messages if you have agreed to receive them, and you can ask us to stop at any time.

3. Our WhatsApp booking assistant

Messages to our WhatsApp line may be answered by an automated assistant that can check availability, make and change bookings and answer questions about our services. It identifies you by the WhatsApp number you message from and will only discuss bookings with the person on that number. Your messages are processed by Meta (WhatsApp) and by the service providers that run the assistant for us. You can ask to speak to a member of our team at any time.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that help us run our business, such as Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform), our website and booking system hosting providers (including Netlify), the providers that power our booking assistant, and Google (analytics and advertising). They may only use your data to provide their services to us.
  • Our staff and drivers, who need it to groom and transport your pet.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

Some of these providers store data outside Singapore. Where that happens, we take steps to make sure your data receives a standard of protection comparable to the PDPA.

5. How long we keep it

We keep client and pet records for as long as you are a client and for a reasonable time afterwards for record-keeping, and as required by law (for example, tax records). Job applications that are not successful are deleted within 12 months. When we no longer need personal data, we delete or anonymise it.

6. How we protect it

We use reasonable security measures, including access controls and encrypted connections, to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, use or loss. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to keep your data safe.

7. Your choices: access, correction and deletion

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how it has been used;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • withdraw your consent to us using your data; or
  • delete your data, including your client record, pet profiles, booking history and WhatsApp conversation records with us.

To request deletion, send a WhatsApp message to 9227 7915 saying "Please delete my data", from the number you used with us, or email charlwong@gmail.com with your name and handphone number. We will confirm who you are, then delete your data within 30 days and let you know when it is done. We may need to keep a small amount of information where the law requires it (for example, invoices), and we will tell you if so. If you withdraw consent or ask for deletion, we may not be able to take further bookings for you.

8. Cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of Google Analytics using Google's browser add-on. Blocking cookies will not stop our website from working.

9. Children

Our services are meant for pet owners aged 18 and above. Applicants under 18 for our courses or jobs should apply with a parent or guardian's consent.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.
